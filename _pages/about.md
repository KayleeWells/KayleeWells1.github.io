About
========
I am an environmental economist and postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Applied Economics and Statistics at the University of Delaware. I use my skills in non-market valuation, big data analysis, and causal inference to provide decision-makers with clearer, data-driven insights. These insights then enable them to make more informed and effective management decisions about natural resources. I’m interested in using innovative methods and datasets to improve decision-making for a wide range of environments and resource uses. The research in my dissertation focused on estimating the value of grassland recreation and restoration in the United States. I'm currently developing an aquatic ecosystem services valuation tool using a machine learning benefit transfer method.

Research Interests
=========
**Primary:** Environmental and Natural Resource Economics 
<br>**Secondary:** Non-Market Valuation, Big Data, Causal Inference, and Environmental Justice
